Before you start — research consent

This is a research tool for a University of North Dakota study on telling human and automated use of AI assistants apart. If you take part, we record, for this session only:

  • the text of the prompts you type into the assistant below,
  • how you type and move the mouse (timing and movement, not screen contents elsewhere),
  • basic connection information (browser type, language, and IP address).

You will be asked to type a short list of prompts that we give you. Some include made-up, fictional business data that we provide for you to paste. Please do not enter any real personal, financial, or confidential information. Taking part is voluntary; you may close the page at any time. Data is used only for research and reported in aggregate.
