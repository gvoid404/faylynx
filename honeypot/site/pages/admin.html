Accuracy over time

One point for the original models and one for each model update, oldest on the left.

    How accuracy is measured

    • Accuracy is the share of entries judged correctly. An entry is one prompt entered on the Prompt Study page, by a participant (links P01 to P20) or by the agent driver.
    • Every figure comes from entries the models were not trained on. The original models are scored on all entries collected so far, so their figures move as more entries come in. Until there are 20 entries, their test scores from before the study are shown instead.
    • The figures of a model update are the estimates made when it was created, and they stay as they were. The behavior model is tested on participants that were left out of its training, the content model on prompts that were left out of its training.
    • The Entries column shows how many entries each row is based on. A figure from few entries can move a lot.

    Update the models from collected data

    Runs on this server. It scores the current models on everything participants and agents have entered, retrains with that data added, and saves the result as a new version. Earlier versions are kept, so an update can always be undone below.
